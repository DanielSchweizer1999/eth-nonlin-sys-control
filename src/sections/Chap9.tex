\section{Chap 9: State-Dependant Riccati Equation}
\subsection{Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR)}
Time continuous system:
\begin{align*}
    \Dot{x} & = Ax + Bu + w   \\
    y(t)    & = Cx + \upsilon
\end{align*}
For a general system with no additive noise $w$ on the state dynamics and perfect knowledge of the state $x$ we can minimize the quadratic cost $J(x_0,u(.)) = \int_0^\infty (x^TQx + u^T R u) dt, \; \forall Q \succ 0, R\succ 0$:
\begin{align*}
    u^\ast(t) & = -R^{-1} B^T P x(t)           \\
    0         & = PA + A^TP + Q - PBR^{-1}B^TP
\end{align*}
\subsection{Observer Design}
If the state is not perfectly known, a state observer is used (Kalman Filter):
\begin{align*}
    \Dot{x}_e & = Ax_e + Bu + L(y-Cx_e)                             \\
    L         & = PC^TR_e^{-1}                                      \\
    0         & = AP + PA^T - PC^TR_e^{-1} CP + Q_e, \; P \succeq 0 \\
    Q_e       & = \mathbb{E}(ww^T)                                  \\
    R_e       & = \mathbb{E}(\upsilon \upsilon^T)
\end{align*}
